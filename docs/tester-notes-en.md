# Skerry – Notes for testers

Thanks for trying Skerry! Skerry is a strategy and action game on the high seas: you command the aircraft carrier Stoorworm, capture islands with amphibious tanks and VTOL jets, build up a supply network and fight the enemy carrier Nuckelavee.

**Version:** It is shown at the bottom of the main menu, for example “Version 0.9.0 · 23/10/2026”. Please include it with every report.

## Getting started

- **In the browser (recommended):** Open the link, ideally in **Chrome** or **Edge** on a Mac or Windows PC. Safari and Firefox have not been tested yet; phones and tablets are not supported. Music and sound start after the first click or key press.
- **As a Mac app:** Unzip the file, drag Skerry into the Applications folder and open it. The “AppleSilicon” file is for Macs with M1 or later, “Intel” for older Macs. On the first start macOS blocks the app because it is not registered with Apple. Then click **“Open Anyway”** under **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

The game generates its island world at start-up; this takes a few seconds.

**Language:** The game starts in English unless your browser or Mac is set to German. The small **“Deutsch”** / **“English”** button at the bottom of the main menu switches the language; the game remembers your choice.

## Playing

- The main menu has a short guide under **How to play** and every key under **Controls**.
- Keyboard and mouse or trackpad are enough. A PlayStation or Xbox controller works too.
- For a first game, the **Easy** difficulty is recommended.
- The game saves by itself every three minutes. You can make your own saves in the menu under **Save**. In the browser the saves stay in that browser; under **Load game → File** you can keep a copy as a file.

## Feedback

What helps most:

- What did you do, what happened, and what did you expect?
- A screenshot (Mac: Cmd+Shift+4, Windows: Win+Shift+S).
- Your computer, operating system and browser (or the app).
- If it stutters: Menu → Settings → Developer → **Performance display** shows the frames per second.

I am especially interested in:

- Is it clear at the start what to do? Where do you get stuck?
- Is the enemy too strong or too weak?
- Does the game run smoothly?
- What is fun, and what is annoying?

## Known limits

- Saves from the browser and from the app are kept separately.
